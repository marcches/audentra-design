# Housing — changes from the review of 2026-08-21

## Agent Brief

**Category:** enhancement (B4.2–B4.4 are conformance gaps against ENR-210/ENR-211, Prioritized)
**Summary:** The student chooses where she will live from a picture, not a monogram; three states stop
rendering as the same page; the page says in every state whether it is asking her for something; the
shortlist is reachable from the catalogue; the rail stops repeating the main column; the catalogue can
be filtered; and the four-answer plan stands, with the story updated to match.

**Current behavior** (confirmed 2026-08-22): residence cards lead with a two-letter monogram
(`.org-tile` initials). The plan offers four answers ("Living on campus", "Commuting", "Arranging my
own housing", "I need help deciding"). `ready`, `empty` and `partial` render identically until the
student picks an answer. Only `onboarding-answered` states that the answer is recorded. There is no
summary panel — **removed on purpose after the Jam of 2026-08-21** (`5e68b80`; the page's comment
says the plan card is the figure and the band the deadline) — and no advisor block, no band. The
catalogue's "Your shortlist is full. Remove one to add this" offers no way to the shortlist. The rail
holds the deadline anchor card (with Housing Services' location, hours and reply time) and one
"What a preference is worth" card that quotes the "Changing your housing answer" guide in full. The
catalogue sorts ("Lowest rate", "Closest") and does not filter. "I need help deciding" routes to the
Help page ("Ask Housing Services"). Residence names render at 15px in two weights; prices and
monograms at 13.5px; "A preference is not an assignment." at 13.5px bold.
