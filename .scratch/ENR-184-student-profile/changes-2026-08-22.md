# Profile — changes from the walkthrough of 2026-08-20, checked against the build of 2026-08-21 and 2026-08-22

## Agent Brief

**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** The student's own photograph replaces the orbit; the page is sectioned rather than
scrolled; the family-permissions block names what is shared and makes revoking deliberate, and says
it is her FERPA right; channel preference stops being one setting; a previous-institution record gets
a home; addresses take US names; and values leave the brand size.

**Current behavior** (confirmed 2026-08-22): the hero is the shared `PageHero` with the `profile`
motif (the orbit). The page is one scroll: two field-group cards ("You", "How Aster reaches you"),
"Who can see your record", and "The rest of your record" (doors to My Documents as a side panel, My
Degree, My Financials). Every editable field is a read state with `Change` (**C1.4 done**). My
Documents has no sidebar row and opens from Profile (**C1.7 done, navigation included**). The
authorization card's legend reads "What Renata can see · 3 of 7" and the seven category names render
beneath as checkboxes; the rail's anchor card repeats "3 of 7 categories, until …". Preferred channel
is one value (Portal / Email / Text message). Addresses are "Term-time address" and "Home address".
No previous-institution section exists. The rail holds "Who can see your record", "Ending your
session" (with the shared-computer sentence), and "Who changes the rest". The page states its own
version ("Profile · Version 4 · Updated …") and "8 of 12 details are yours". Values render at 15px in
weights 600 and 700 with no rule.

**Desired behavior:**

1. **C1.1 — her photograph, where the orbit is.** The hero's figure slot seats the student's own
   photograph (`maya-johnson.webp` is on file; the rule is `<Avatar person>`); where none is uploaded,
   her initials in the monogram treatment and an upload control. This is a `PageHero` variant that
   seats a person in the motif's place — the one place in the portal where a photograph of the student
   appears, uploaded by her. Nothing about club or residence imagery changes.
2. **C1.3 — sectioned, not scrolled**, the way My Financials is (tabs under one hero): *About me*
   (name, preferred name, pronouns, the photograph) · *Contact and communication* (addresses, phone,
   email, channel preferences) · *Who can see what* (family authorizations) · *My documents* (what she
   has sent — the existing panel's content as a section) · *Where I came from* (C1.8). The version
   line and "8 of 12 details are yours" stay (C11.4).
3. **C1.5, C10.1 — name what is shared; make revoking deliberate; say whose right it is.** The count
   heading becomes a statement followed by the category names ("8 of 12 details are yours" is the
   model: a count works when what it counts follows); revoking asks for confirmation that names the
   person and what they will stop seeing; the seven categories stay the onboarding's. The section
   says, in one line, that these are her education records and that sharing them is her choice to make
   and to withdraw (FERPA — C10.1); the rail's duplicated count goes (C1.2 as narrowed by C11.1).
4. **C1.2 — the rail.** Keep the session card (ENR-179 AC7; the shared-computer sentence is a real
   risk stated in her terms) and the offices card; drop only the category count duplicated from the
   section. Nothing else in the rail changes in this card.
5. **C1.6 — channel preference: one default, per-category overrides** (decided 2026-08-22). The
   onboarding keeps asking one value and it is the default; in *Contact and communication*, behind a
   closed disclosure ("Set a different channel for one kind of message" or close to it), one row per
   checklist category with the default pre-selected and the three channels to choose from. Not a
   grid. The categories are the checklist's (post-rename: Your offer · Your financials · Your health ·
   Your details · Your campus life · Your degree).
6. **C1.8 — "Where I came from", owned here, read by My Degree** (decided 2026-08-22): the previous
   institution(s) — the high school, and any college or programme she transferred from (the data has
   a final high school transcript requirement, an IB Diploma transcript and a Northside College
   transcript) — with coursework, grades and credits, presented as a record rather than a form,
   shown as received with each transcript's review state. A potential match's "See the evidence" on
   My Degree opens this record at the line it cites (ENR-188 card wires its side).
7. **C1.9 — US address labels.** "Term-time address" → "Campus address"; "Home address" → "Permanent
   address"; add "Mailing address" where it differs from the permanent one.
8. **C11.3 — values leave the brand size.** Values take `--fs-h2`, at `--fw-regular` for a value the
   student can change and `--fw-semi` for one the institution owns, so weight carries the distinction
   the grouping already makes. 15px appears only on the sidebar brand line.

**Key interfaces:** `PageHero` gains a person-figure variant; `PageShell` tabs for the sections; the
authorization legend and the revoke flow (a confirmation `Drawer`/`InfoModal` naming person and
categories); the channel preference data shape becomes per category; a previous-institution record
shape in the profile data; address field labels.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- [ ] The hero shows the student's photograph, or her initials with an upload control; the orbit does
      not render on Profile (C1.1).
- [ ] Profile is sectioned under one hero and no section needs more than one screen at 1440×900;
      "Profile · Version …" and "8 of 12 details are yours" remain (C1.3, C11.4).
- [ ] The authorization states the shared categories by name, not a count; revoking asks confirmation
      naming the person and the categories; the FERPA sentence is present; the rail no longer repeats
      the count; the session card remains (C1.2, C1.5, C10.1).
- [ ] Channel preference has one default (the onboarding's answer) and a per-category override for
      every checklist category, closed by default (C1.6).
- [ ] A "Where I came from" section exists with institution, coursework, grades and credits, as a
      record with each transcript's review state; My Degree's "See the evidence" can open it at a
      line (C1.8).
- [ ] Addresses read "Campus address", "Permanent address" and, where different, "Mailing address"
      (C1.9).
- [ ] No value on the screen renders at 15px; student-owned values are `--fw-regular`, institution-owned
      `--fw-semi` (C11.3).
- [ ] Editing still takes one explicit step (C1.4 preserved); `npm run build` passes; 1440 and 390.

**Out of scope:** My Documents' behaviour (ENR-146 stories; only its home changed, and that is done);
family-permission *permissions* (the backend already allows the edit); the session card's removal (no).

**Domain:** FERPA is the frame for "Who can see what" — read `docs/domain/us-enrollment.md` (being
written 2026-08-22) for the record categories and the wording a US registrar uses, and keep the seven
categories the onboarding already names; the three addresses (campus, permanent, mailing) are what a
US student sees on every registrar form (C1.9).
