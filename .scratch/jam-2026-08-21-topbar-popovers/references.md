# References — the two topbar popovers

Three searches, `platform: web`, `mode: deep`, 2026-08-21.

## Notifications panel — the overall layout

- [Dovetail — notifications](https://mobbin.com/screens/3d315db6-ea16-4346-9cd2-4fd025da5f70) — every row
  opens with the *source's mark*, the title is one line, the time is small, and the unread mark is a dot
  at the row's trailing edge. **Taken:** a mark on every row; the unread dot lives in the trailing cell,
  not before the title.
- [Slite — updates](https://mobbin.com/screens/fb60bed4-2dd5-4221-ba5d-c1fc3fef0f1a) — the feed is ranked
  into *Where you're needed* and *Nice to see* before it is listed. **Taken:** the two runs, labelled once;
  the needs-you run is the one that may spend colour.
- [Jira — notifications](https://mobbin.com/screens/f7eb5777-1125-4f47-bdcc-39931e19cfeb) — unread rows
  are the heavy ones; read rows fall back to regular weight. **Taken:** read state is weight, not only a
  dot.
- [Remote — inbox](https://mobbin.com/screens/5e916e4a-4c86-4c06-a455-9a8d1785603d) — tinted tiles by
  notification type. **Taken** in spirit (a tile per row), **rejected** in palette: one colour per row
  makes five colours in one list. Here only the needs-you row's tile is tinted.

## Points popover — the balance and what it reaches

- [sweetgreen — rewards](https://mobbin.com/screens/11084818-1bd6-4d14-bd40-b3baa6311e28) — a ladder
  with thresholds marked on a track, each threshold named under its mark. **Taken:** the thresholds are
  marks on the track *and* rows under it (the track is too short to carry four labels).
- [Gamma — account balance](https://mobbin.com/screens/0a2f0470-91a6-40ca-bd64-6a6ac4c7812b) — the popover
  opens with the figure (the balance) set large, then the ways to earn more. **Taken:** the figure leads.
- [Duolingo — streak goal](https://mobbin.com/screens/0c69d037-f505-4dd6-a961-76127f55baaf) — locked
  rewards as cards with a lock. **Rejected:** one card per reward is a box in a box, and redemption
  mechanics are out of scope (ENR-148).

## Rejected across both

- Tabs (*All · Unread*, *Personal · Friends*) — the product's one disclosure is the status head and the
  popover has no room for a second navigation. Ranking the feed into two runs does the tabs' job without
  asking the student to switch.
- Avatars on notification rows (Jira, Slite, Qatalog) — a notification here comes from an *office*, and
  an office is a name, never a face (design-workflow, People).
