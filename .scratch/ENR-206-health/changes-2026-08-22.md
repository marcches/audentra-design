# Health — a new name, and one typography item to verify, not assume

## Agent Brief

**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** The section is named "My Health and Wellness" in the navigation and the hero, aligning it
with the "My …" form and ending the reading that it is only a medical record. One measured defect from
9.3 is probably already gone after the 08-21 changes and must be verified in the browser before
anything is built.

**Current behavior** (confirmed 2026-08-22): the navigation row and the hero kicker read "Health"; the
hero title "One record, and one question." After `cc2dd21` the accessibility question is an `EntryRow`
in a headless card (title "Accessibility" as the row's strong, note "One question that's yours to
answer, or not."), and the immunization status is the shared `StatusPill` at `--fs-small` — not the
8.5px the 9.3 measurement recorded.

**Desired behavior:**

1. **C9 — the name.** "My Health and Wellness" in the navigation row and the hero kicker (title case,
   per the navigation convention and ENR-174 AC5 — decided 2026-08-22, `ledger.md` §5.7). The hero
   title and lede stay; the screen is already one page with two things (H4). **In the same commit,
   the other hero kickers align to their navigation label** (ENR-174 AC4, one concept one name; one
   file, `navigation.js`): "Academic · BA Computer Science" → "My Degree · BA Computer Science",
   "Campus life · Published by Aster Student Life" → "My Campus Life · Published by Aster Student
   Life"; Appointments and My Financials already match; My Enrollment's "Class of 2031" with its
   "Offer accepted" flag stays — it is the one hero that states her standing rather than the
   section's name, by design.
2. **9.3 — verify.** Measure "Accessibility" and "Immunization record" in the browser at 1440 and 390.
   If the 08-21 design (a summary panel for two things, the question as a row) holds, the two are
   peers by construction and 9.3's "make it an H2" is moot — record that in this file. If the
   accessibility title still renders as bold body size under a larger sibling, take the section
   treatment. No status may render at `--fs-micro` (already true — confirm).
3. **ENR-212, for the record:** if the screen ever shows what happens after a yes, the fact to show is
   that a yes creates a work item for Accessibility Services with a contact deadline derived from the
   start of term, carrying no condition. Not built here; noted so nobody invents a different sentence.
