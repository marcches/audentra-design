# References — one page shell for every section

Three searches, `platform: web`, `mode: deep`. The second one changed the design.

## Layout — a band with a summary card tucked under its lower edge

- [Later — Organic Social Overview](https://mobbin.com/screens/fd51fa4e-8aef-48c0-ab92-21a0436f41d7) —
  a saturated band across the top, the page title and its headline figures immediately under it. We
  take the relationship: the band says where you are, the strip under it says how you stand.
- [Evernote — Your Home](https://mobbin.com/screens/519dddb4-ce43-4db9-8d36-63b93107b6f5) — a
  full-bleed gradient band with the page title sitting on the boundary. Confirms the tuck reads as
  deliberate rather than as a collision, which is exactly what the Jam said ours did not.

## The hard part — a leaf switcher on a coloured hero

**Searched for it; it does not exist in the corpus.** Every result put the tab row on the page
background, above the content it switches, never inside a coloured band:

- [Stripe — Business settings](https://mobbin.com/screens/cfd50149-0fe1-44c6-b7ee-6784c5519677)
- [Patreon — Edit page](https://mobbin.com/screens/30763547-4fe8-4b9b-99b0-9d0708363774)
- [Reddit — Settings](https://mobbin.com/screens/80cd19d1-0674-4f7d-bea2-a23c6dc8b1d4)
- [Mixpanel — Access Security](https://mobbin.com/screens/c46926cd-0504-4499-9124-b3d0201b8b95)
- [Cloudflare — Configurations](https://mobbin.com/screens/cdb5cd58-ecce-412a-8c39-318198c7e31d)

This rejected the first plan, which put the tabs inside the band.

## What replaced it — summary above, tabs below

- [Rox — Billing & Usage](https://mobbin.com/screens/d05a2f5e-1bfb-421f-8723-dd288e3c92dd) — **the
  shape we took.** A summary strip of facts at the top, *then* the tab row, then only what the tabs
  switch. Our own code already argued for this: the balance appears on all three financials leaves,
  so a tab row above it would promise a change that never happens.
- [Hume AI — Billing](https://mobbin.com/screens/2863076b-5a75-441b-93b5-1dbce3f4b788) — the same
  order with two tab levels; confirms the rule survives nesting.
- [WRITER — Billing](https://mobbin.com/screens/136612da-f0a2-4c63-9e6b-2d7e7a076a36) and
  [OpenAI Platform — Billing](https://mobbin.com/screens/d684cd6c-febe-441f-a6cd-de43bd19eb6a) —
  both put the balance figure *under* the tabs. Rejected: their balance is genuinely per-tab and
  ours is not.

## Rejected

- [Base44 — FinFlow dashboard](https://mobbin.com/screens/65c6cccb-553e-44d1-b328-918b5cb779f9) —
  gradient card holding the primary figure inside the band. Tempting, but it would state the balance
  twice, once in the band and once in the panel — ENR-174 AC4.
