# References — the one new screen, `#/accessibility`

Every other change in this card is copy or data on a screen that already has its own research. The
Accessibility route is the one screen the document creates, and it is a *move*: the question card
that ENR-206 designed inside Health, now on a page of its own. So the card's own references stand —
[YNAB Support Access Mode](https://mobbin.com/screens/7f14f6d6-2262-46ee-899c-cbe02b2884a3) for the
two-door question with the declining option written in full, [Wellfound](https://mobbin.com/screens/c2aecfb8-828f-4d20-94c7-728fdc0ad0d7)
for stating the current answer and keeping the other door visible (see
`.scratch/ENR-206-health/references.md`) — and the two searches below are about the page around it.

## 1. A page that is one sensitive, optional question

Query: *university student portal accessibility services page asking whether the student wants to
request accommodations, two radio options, note stating who can see the answer* · `web` · deep

- [Glassdoor — Help end inequality (optional)](https://mobbin.com/screens/e2bbdf56-72f2-4580-b9c8-86bf32202a87)
  — **the page shape we take.** A standalone page whose whole job is an optional, sensitive
  question; the heading says *(optional)* in its own words, the intro says what is done with the
  answer and who does not get it, each question offers a real "prefer not to say", and the right
  column explains the rules rather than selling the form. That is our hero + card + rail: the
  question, the *seen by* line on the card, and a rail that names the one office and says what it
  decides (nothing).
- [Instagram — Sensitive content control](https://mobbin.com/screens/c2d632da-17ec-4ecc-92cb-632214761015)
  — one card, radio options each carrying its consequence line, alone in the main column under its
  own left-nav row. Confirms that a page with a single card is a page, not an empty state — and that
  *Accessibility* sits in a sidebar as a sibling row, which is where the document puts ours.

### Rejected

- [Expedia — Basic information → Accessibility needs](https://mobbin.com/screens/a3cdee26-8825-4f24-b46f-246fb25ce7e6)
  — accommodations as one dropdown inside a personal-data form. This is precisely the filing the
  document objects to (6.5): the question becomes a field among fields, and the page around it is
  about the person's data, not about a conversation.
- [Zoho CRM — Accessibility settings](https://mobbin.com/screens/ca0ed8ed-5eec-49fb-9ad4-01b4d558bac5)
  — *Accessibility* as display toggles (reading focus, underline links). Same word, different
  thing; our page must not read as preferences.
- [Hims — How does your skin react](https://mobbin.com/screens/806bc16d-c994-4546-b4cb-0e799963486e)
  — a wizard step: one question full-screen with a Back arrow. Gives the answer the weight of a
  form; ENR-206 rejected the same shape in GoFundMe for the same reason.

## 2. A single card with a rail that names who receives it

Query: *settings page whose main column holds a single privacy question card with two choices, and a
right sidebar explaining which team receives the answer and that nothing else changes* · `web` · deep

- Instagram again (above) — the nearest match; nothing returned carried a rail. So the rail follows
  this repo's own rule (anchor card first, then light cards) using the vocabulary Health already has:
  the office's usual reply time as the anchor, the *Who is on the other side* card under it.

### Rejected

- [Revolut Business — Privacy](https://mobbin.com/screens/397f4a53-d8c2-4bea-b8a9-bf27870d264c),
  [Mixpanel — Data & Privacy](https://mobbin.com/screens/6510ae38-d5e2-4acf-9bd3-0e844aee9bb8)
  — toggles. Two positions cannot show *never answered* as distinct from *answered no*.
- [Threads — Privacy](https://mobbin.com/screens/571dca6f-03d2-488d-9842-88169b22c8a6) — a
  choice presented as a gate with a Next button; ours must cost nothing to leave unanswered.
