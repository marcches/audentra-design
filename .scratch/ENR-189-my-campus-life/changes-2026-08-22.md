# My Campus Life — changes from the review of 2026-08-21

## Agent Brief

**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** The only mandatory thing on the screen sits above the tabs in every viewport and says what
to do about it; the Events tab shows one list at a time; rows that state what to do offer the means;
club cards get an emblem, a way to show interest (because it feeds recommendations) and a way to reach
the named person (through Edward); academic clubs divide by school; a self-evident match label is
suppressed; and the off-scale sizes go.

**Current behavior** (confirmed 2026-08-22): `RequiredStrip` ("Required for you", crimson head,
`--fs-copy` bold) renders above the tabs under 1061px and as the first thing in the rail at or above
it, only when a session is required; its rows state date, time, consequence and office and offer
nothing. The Events tab shows the view switch (For you / Everything / Past) inside the status
heading, a filter row with category chips and the count, then two groups — "Picked for your
interests {n}" and "Everything else at Aster" by week — and "Show more". Every club row is one
button with no inner actions; its tile shows a duotone icon if published, else initials; the match
chip reads "Matches {interest}" on clubs and events alike. Event and club titles are 15px/700 under a
17px/600 section title; the required heading is 11.5px/700 above 13.5px/700 rows; the interests line
in the rail is 15px/700.

**Desired behavior**, in the document's execution order, with the Part B/C patches folded in:

1. **C3 — one list.** The view switch drives the whole list. "For you" shows the matching events and
   the count reads "{n} events match your interests"; "Everything" shows all and the count reads "{n}
   events". "Picked for your interests" goes; "Everything else at Aster" becomes a line at the end of
   the "For you" list: "Not what you're looking for? See everything Aster has published." (copy §6.2).
2. **C1, C6, C7 — the required block.** Required sessions sit in the main column **above the tabs in
   every viewport** (the viewport switch goes; design-workflow already states why: an obligation must
   not hide behind a tab), visually distinct from published events because they come from another
   office and are not a choice (rule 3). The rail card is not re-created. Heading at the section
   treatment (`--fs-h2`/`--fw-semi`) with the support line "These come from other offices. They aren't
   campus life, and they aren't optional."; session titles at the row treatment
   (`--fs-h2`/`--fw-regular`). In `full-board` the three sessions from two offices stay on their own
   rows and the banner's count keeps counting ("3 sessions are required").
3. **C2 — required sessions have an action.** Each carries the same RSVP line the events use ("RSVP by
   email" / "Book a shift" / "No RSVP needed") and "Add to calendar" (copy §6.1).
4. **C4 as amended, C4b, C7, B5.2 — rows act, and clubs have a face that is not a face.**
   - *Events*: a bookable event takes a control with its words ("Book a shift"); an event by email
     takes "Email the host", which opens a message to the named host (registration is a resolution —
     it stays direct, B5.2); an external booking links out and says so; "No RSVP needed" stays a
     label. Events take **"Follow"** (C7): an event is dated, so following it is about being reminded.
   - *Clubs*: the leading slot carries a **published emblem** at the size and radius the Housing
     monogram uses, falling back to the club's initials — never a photograph of people, never stock,
     never generated, in any state (C4b, C8.1). **Needs-info on the assets** (`ledger.md` §5.3):
     build the slot and the fallback now. The contact control names the person ("Message Dana") and
     **opens Edward with the question written and unsent** (Part A §12.3–12.4: "How do I get in touch
     with Dana Whitfield about the Aster Chamber Choir?") — not a direct message. The primary action
     **"I'm interested"** returns (C7) and says what it is: it feeds what the portal shows her first
     (ENR-187 AC5 extended), it is not an application to join, whether the club is told is a separate
     decision the call did not make, and it changes nothing about her interests, progress or points.
     The campus document's §6.3 sentence "Student Life passes your name to the club…" is **not
     used** — C7 supersedes it. Propose copy in the spirit of "This shapes what we show you first. It
     isn't an application, and it doesn't change your interests or your points." and confirm it in Jam.
   - Until the Edward door exists (ENR-181), the contact control may stay a plain control to the
     named person rather than route nowhere; the acceptance is against the door.
5. **C8.2, C8.3 — categories and the match label.** The "Academic" category is replaced by the
   institution's schools, published by Student Life like every category (data change + chips). The
   match label is suppressed where the club's own category is the interest that matched it ("Music" on
   a music club says nothing) and kept where the match is not obvious.
6. **C5, C8 — the remaining sizes.** Event and club titles `--fs-h2`/`--fw-regular`; the interests
   line in the rail `--fs-body`. 15px appears only on the sidebar brand line.
7. **C9, C10 — verify.** No figure leads on this screen and none should (counts are labels, not
   summary figures). Nothing collapses; "Show more" is pagination. `partial` (events loaded, clubs
   did not) is the one state no other screen has — check it (E3).

**Key interfaces:** the required block is one component above the tabs (the `notice` slot or the main
column head — the shell decides the position, the page passes the block), with rows that carry the
RSVP line and the calendar action; event rows and the club row stop being one button and carry
actions; the club tile gains an emblem slot with the initials fallback (the shape Housing's residence
card also takes); the interest signal is recorded where recommendations read it; the categories are
data; the contact control calls the Edward door.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- [ ] The tab shows one list at a time and the count names which list it belongs to (C3).
- [ ] In every state and every viewport a required session appears above the tab content and never in
      the rail (C1).
- [ ] No required session renders without stating what the student has to do about it and offering
      "Add to calendar" (C2).
- [ ] No row states what the student should do without offering the means; "No RSVP needed" stays a
      label (C4).
- [ ] Every club card shows a published emblem or an initials fallback; no card shows a photograph of
      a person; event cards keep their date block (C4b, C8.1).
- [ ] The club contact control opens Edward with the §12.4 question written, unsent, focused (§12).
- [ ] "I'm interested" exists, says it feeds what she is shown first and is not an application, and
      changes nothing about interests, progress or points; events offer "Follow" (C7).
- [ ] "Academic" no longer appears as a category; the schools do (C8.2).
- [ ] A match label never repeats the club's own category (C8.3).
- [ ] No title on the screen renders at 15px; no row title is heavier than its section; the required
      heading matches every other section heading; 13.5px appears only on navigation (C5–C8).
- [ ] Nothing on the screen collapses; no figure leads (C9, C10).
- [ ] `npm run build` passes; 1440 and 390 checked; `ready`, `full-board` and `partial` inspected.

**Out of scope:** a recommendation engine (the signal is recorded; ranking stays disclosed as it is);
telling the club (not asked); the Housing monogram/image work (ENR-207); building the Edward door
(ENR-181); the rail's interests and provenance cards (unchanged).

**What is right and must survive** (§3): the screen refuses to be a feed; reading changes nothing, and
it says so; matching is disclosed; every club names a person; clubs carry a dated latest update; RSVP
requirements are stated per event.

**Domain:** Aster Student Life publishes everything here and the portal only shows it (rule 2);
required sessions belong to other offices (Office of the Registrar, Housing Services) and keep their
office and consequence lines. The schools that replace "Academic" are Aster's — read
`docs/domain/aster.md` (being written 2026-08-22) for their names before inventing any; and "RSVP",
"orientation", "club" follow US campus usage per `docs/domain/us-enrollment.md`.
