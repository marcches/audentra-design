# References — Jam of 2026-08-21, My Enrollment (Laura) + Marco's standardisation brief

Four searches, `platform: web`, `mode: deep`. Screens looked at, not just read.

## Collapsible groups on a checklist (J1)
- [Remote — Get started with Recruit](https://mobbin.com/screens/ed95183c-7542-4966-87a7-581bb02b72b9) —
  each group is one card; the head is a full-width row with an icon tile, the name, a state chip at
  the trailing edge and a chevron; locked rows carry a lock glyph in the trailing slot. **We take**
  the head-as-button with the count at the trailing edge and the rows inside the same card.
- [Vanta — Finish Starter Guide](https://mobbin.com/screens/d6c8a960-3253-4ca4-b4aa-06e902fa0e4e) —
  group cards with "4 of 4 complete" and a chevron, open group shows its rows in place. **We take**
  the count sitting before the chevron, and the open/closed state read from the chevron alone.
- [Langdock — Get started](https://mobbin.com/screens/79f9a652-a8b4-4e7a-afcc-f9a22153b2de) — the
  same shape with a progress ring per group. **Rejected**: a ring per group says the groups are
  comparable; *with Aster* and *coming up later* are not things she completes.
- [Squarespace — Get started](https://mobbin.com/screens/56e91ea0-90b5-483d-b7d3-f1a8ad276f3f) —
  collapsed groups with count and a progress bar. Same rejection.

## Loading that can be seen (J5)
- [Coda — setting up](https://mobbin.com/screens/55171e3f-c462-477b-b92c-843a8fd8c9b2) — a
  spinner and one line, "Setting up your first doc…", centred; the sidebar is a skeleton. **We
  take** the spinner-with-a-sentence as the one visible signal, and keep the skeleton around it.
- [Zoho CRM — loading](https://mobbin.com/screens/e42b6c72-0dcf-49b5-9a77-af71bf04c022) — mark +
  "Loading" label. Same idea, heavier.
- [Neon — skeleton](https://mobbin.com/screens/9b5b1374-c015-495b-abcb-9cce659c741b),
  [Frame — skeleton](https://mobbin.com/screens/482086dd-4487-417a-b11b-fcbb798e87e4) — skeleton
  only, no motion, no word. **Rejected**: this is exactly what Laura said did not read as loading.

## The error, centred and red (J7)

  nothing else about the bar changing.
