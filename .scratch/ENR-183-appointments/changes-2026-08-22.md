# Appointments — the asking leaves the screen

## Agent Brief

**Category:** bug (the prototype drifted from ENR-178, Prioritized — Part A §8.2) and enhancement (the
callback, the copy)
**Summary:** Appointments keeps its job — book from posted times, follow what is booked — and loses
the asking: the time request built on 2026-08-21 goes, the no-times row opens Edward with the question
written, the callback is offered from Edward's prompt and its drawer lives here, and the rejected
strings become US campus English.

**Current behavior** (confirmed 2026-08-22, built in `ebd42f0` from the 08-21 document and ADR 0005):
the booking drawer has two tabs, "Published times" and "Ask for a time". A topic with no times reads
"No times published yet" / "They haven't opened a calendar yet. You can ask for a time, or wait and
we'll tell you when they open one." with primary "Ask for a time" and secondary "Tell me when times
open"; a topic with times reads "Choose a time" with a text link "Ask for another time"; the band
above a closed topic reads "{Team} hasn't opened a calendar yet". The `no-times` empty state offers
"Ask a team for a time" into the drawer's ask tab. The rail and the card head open "How booking
works". A request shows as REQUESTED (`state: 'requested'`, `window`, `subject`, `requestedOn`).

**Desired behavior:**

1. **No time request (ADR 0008, §4, §8.2).** The drawer keeps one tab, the published times. "Ask for
   a time" and "Ask for another time" leave every row. Where times are posted the primary is
   **"Book a time"** and there is no fallback to offer. A2 is superseded; A7 narrows to the callback.
2. **The no-times row (§6.1 as reconciled with §12 — `ledger.md` §3.1).** State "No times posted";
   explanation "{Office} hasn't posted times yet. You can ask them to call you back, or we'll let you
   know when times go up."; the primary control **opens Edward with the input filled and unsent**:
   "Academic Advising hasn't posted any times. How do I get in touch with them?" (§12.4); the
   secondary stays on the row as **"Email me when times are posted"** (a notification, not a route to a
   person). The band for a closed topic reads "{Office} doesn't have times posted right now" and its
   case 3 opens Edward the same way (A6 amended). Offices are named as the campus names them:
   "Academic Advising", without "Office", in running text.
3. **The `no-times` empty state** keeps its distinction from "no appointments" (ENR-183's own brief;
   "different emptinesses") and its action opens Edward with "No team has posted times yet. Who
   should I talk to about my enrollment step?" (§12.3–12.4).
4. **The callback request (§6.2 corrected by §8.3, §7.2).** Reached from Edward's escalation prompt,
   never from the row. Drawer "REQUEST A CALLBACK" / "Ask {Office} to call you" / "They don't have
   times posted right now. Tell them when you're usually free and they'll get back to you." / field
   "When are you usually free?" with help "Days and rough times are enough." / the about field as in
   the booking drawer / note "This isn't a booking. Someone from {Office} gets in touch, usually within
   {the reply time that office publishes}." / "Send request". The resulting item: badge **CALLBACK
   REQUESTED**, support "Waiting on {Office}. You'll see their reply here."; it exposes no assignee or
   team detail; the reply arrives in the portal and email notifies (§8.1). The `requested` state and
   its shape become the callback's (ADR 0008).
5. **"How booking works" → "How this works"** (§6.1), in the rail, the card head and the `InfoModal`
   title, matching the reference screen's label.
6. **Category labels** follow the rename (§6.3) by construction — the strings live in the enrollment
   data (ENR-164 card); `conversationTypes[].category` must keep matching them.
7. **Everything else in `appointments-changes-2026-08-21.md` stands** — typography (9.1 confirms it
   conforms), hierarchy, the removal of the metric card (done), the accordion rules, the states and
   their copy, the row anatomy, the booking drawer's published-times tab.
8. **Periodic advisor meetings (§5, §7.3)** are out of MVP and mock only: an institution-scheduled
   meeting the student confirms, reschedules or declines, sharing the appointment card and none of
   the booking flow. Not built in this card — a `/prototype` on its own branch, pointed at from the
   story a human opens.

**Key interfaces:** `BookingDrawer` (one tab); `TopicRow` actions and copy; the band rule; the
`no-times` `StateCard` action; a callback `Drawer` opened by the Edward prompt with the topic and
subject passed in; the appointment state named for the callback; the `InfoModal` title.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- [ ] No control anywhere on the screen lets the student propose a time; the drawer has one tab
      (ENR-178 AC1, ADR 0008).
- [ ] A topic with times reads "Book a time" and offers no fallback; a topic without reads "No times
      posted", explains it in the §6.1 words, opens Edward filled and unsent, and offers "Email me when
      times are posted".
- [ ] The band for a closed topic reads "{Office} doesn't have times posted right now" and opens Edward.
- [ ] The `no-times` empty state reads differently from "no appointments" and opens Edward with the
      §12.4 question.
- [ ] The callback drawer opens from Edward's prompt with the §6.2 copy and the office's published
      reply time; the item reads CALLBACK REQUESTED with the §6.2 support line and no team detail.
- [ ] "How this works" replaces "How booking works" everywhere on the screen and in the modal.
- [ ] No string says "opened a calendar" or "the {X} Office" in running text.
- [ ] `npm run build` passes; every state in the 08-21 document re-inspected at 1440 and 390.

**Out of scope:** the Edward door and prompt themselves (ENR-181); the Help page; live chat; building
the periodic-meeting mock in this card; the rename's source strings (ENR-164).

**Domain:** "posted times" and "has availability" are how a US student hears it; "Academic Advising"
is the office's running-text name; reply times are published per office (Housing Services "2 business
days", Health Services "5 business days" already do) — read `docs/domain/us-enrollment.md` and
`docs/domain/aster.md` (being written 2026-08-22) before naming an office or a window.

**Blocked by:** ENR-181 (the door and the prompt — the row can open Edward only once it exists);
ADR 0008 (done, this triage); `../review-2026-08-21/brief.md` where the badge borrows the status
vocabulary.
