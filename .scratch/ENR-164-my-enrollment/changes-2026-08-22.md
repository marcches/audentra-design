# My Enrollment — dates that say what they are, lighter completed rows, and the category labels the whole portal reads

## Agent Brief

**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** The reference screen was praised as a whole (C12) — touch it lightly. Three asks from the
walkthrough, one rename that the checklist owns for the whole portal, and the seat for the inline
Edward control once it exists.

**Current behavior** (confirmed 2026-08-22): completed rows show a figure ("150") and a date ("Aug
7") and explain neither; they keep their description; completed and upcoming rows do not carry the
category the active rows carry. The category labels — "Your offer", "Money and aid", "About you",
"Health and wellness", "Campus life", "Your degree" — live once, in the enrollment data, and
Appointments derives its categories from them and matches them on its conversation types.

**Desired behavior:**

1. **C2.1 — every date says what kind of date it is, every figure what it is.** A completed row reads
   as one sentence: what it was, when it completed, what it earned. An upcoming row says whether a
   date is due or coming up ("Is it due, or is it coming up?").
2. **C2.2 — a completed row drops its description** and keeps title, category, completion date, points.
3. **C2.3 — completed and upcoming rows carry the category**, as active rows do; the category is what
   ties a checklist item to the screen that owns it.
4. **§6.3 — the rename, at the source.** "Your offer" (unchanged), "Money and aid" → "Your financials",
   "Health and wellness" → "Your health", "About you" → "Your details", "Campus life" → "Your campus
   life", "Your degree" (unchanged). One change in the enrollment data; Appointments' conversation
   types keep matching (same commit, by construction — the document requires the two screens change
   in one release). The navigation keeps "My …" deliberately: navigation is her filing, content
   addressed to her says "Your" — recorded so it reads as a decision, not an accident (a line in
   `navigation.js`'s header comment or `CONTEXT.md`).
5. **C5 — the seat for Edward at the task** (after ENR-181): a task row can carry the quiet control that
   opens Edward with the task's context loaded and the question written, unsent. This card hosts the
   control once the pattern exists; it does not build the pattern.

**Key interfaces:** the task row's completed/upcoming variants; the category strings in the enrollment
data and everything that reads them (Appointments' `conversationTypes[].category`); the task row's
trailing cell for the Edward control.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- [ ] No date on a task is ambiguous about its kind; no figure is unexplained (C2.1).
- [ ] Completed rows show title, category, completion date and points, and no description (C2.2).
- [ ] Every row — active, completed, upcoming — shows its category (C2.3).
- [ ] The old labels appear nowhere in `src/`; the new ones appear on the checklist and on Appointments
      in the same commit; the "My/Your" split is recorded (§6.3).
- [ ] Nothing else on the screen changed (C12); `npm run build` passes; 1440 and 390.

**Out of scope:** anything the 08-21 Jam settled; the Edward pattern itself (ENR-181); the semantic
status colours (foundations — this screen only consumes them).

**Domain:** the categories name the parts of a US enrollment journey the checklist is organised by;
read `docs/domain/us-enrollment.md` (being written 2026-08-22) before adding or renaming one beyond
§6.3.

**Blocked by:** nothing for 1–4 (this is wave 1c of `ledger.md` §6); ENR-181 for 5.
