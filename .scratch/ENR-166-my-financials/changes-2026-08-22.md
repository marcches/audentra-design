# My Financials — changes from the review of 2026-08-21

## Agent Brief

**Category:** enhancement (F2 as corrected by B3.1 is a conformance fix against ENR-160)
**Summary:** One framing for the payments, one label for the balance, every changeable figure marked,
the repeats removed by weight rather than deletion, the alert replaced by the band, and the figure
hierarchy righted — so the first block of each tab is visible without scrolling and the largest number
on a tab is the one the tab is about.

**Current behavior** (confirmed in the tree on 2026-08-22): Overview's rail says "Payment 1 of 5" and
"$29,160 scheduled across 5 payments this year…"; Payments says "You're on the 4-month plan" with the
deposit listed above four installments. The same $32,400 is labelled "Estimated remaining balance" in
the shared card and the table and "Still to cover, before your pending loan" in the Overview figures.
"Possible additional aid" renders on Overview (table group) and on Financial aid (a section). Each
"Documents that need you" row carries an "Open it" button and the pending loan row carries "Verify
income", while the shared alert carries the same task and action on all three tabs. Installment rows
read SCHEDULED with an exact figure; the estimate line is under the block. The progress preview is
three rows of NOT STARTED YET. The outstanding-work alert is `AlertStrip` — a `Notice` docked in the
summary foot — not `ActionBand`. The Housing and Meals rows carry "Compare residences" and an
assumption line. Coverage figures are body size; the $500 deposit is the largest type on the tab.

**Desired behavior**, in the document's execution order:

1. **F3, F1 — names first.** One label for the balance everywhere: "Estimated remaining balance"; the
   pending-loan qualifier moves into the note under the table (§6.1). One framing for payments on
   both tabs: the deposit is a separate payment before the plan starts, and the plan is four
   installments (§6.2: "Your deposit, before the plan starts"; "$29,160 this year: your $500 deposit,
   then four installments. The installments are estimates and are recalculated if your aid changes.").
2. **F5, F8 — mark what can change.** Installment rows carry the same ESTIMATE marker the balance
   uses ("SCHEDULED · ESTIMATE"); the deposit does not. Per B3.2's last paragraph and C10.2, the marker
   says what changes it — "recalculated if your aid changes" — as a tooltip on the marker or beside
   the figure, not only in the footnote (a hint may repeat the footnote; if it says more than the page
   already says, it is an `InfoTip`). Housing and Meals rows are marked "IF YOU KEEP THIS PLAN" and
   their links name the effect: "See what a different room costs" / "See what a different plan costs".
   **B4.5**: the Housing figure is derived from the student's current housing answer and the screen
   says so; changing the answer changes the figure; neither screen holds its own housing number.
3. **F2 as corrected by B3.1, and F4 — the repeats, by weight.** The band (step 6) carries the action.
   The "Documents that need you" block **stays in every state**, carries its count, and reconciles with
   the enrollment checklist (ENR-160 AC5); its rows lose the duplicate action button and state the
   document, its office, its deadline and its consequence; when nothing is outstanding the block
   renders a state saying so (ENR-160 AC4). The pending loan row on Financial aid keeps "waiting on
   your income verification" and loses its button. "Possible additional aid" lives on Financial aid
   only; Overview keeps its one table row with a link to the tab.
4. **F9 — one obligation, one record.** The documents here state that they are checklist items and
   show the same due date and the same action label the checklist uses; not the points or the time
   estimate.
5. **F6 — the preview before there is data** is the sentence, the date of the first check, and the
   three rules one line each with no value column (§6.3); the table appears when there is something
   in it.
6. **F7 — the band.** The shared alert becomes `ActionBand` in the reference screen's position with
   this screen's rule: a document outstanding → the nearest by due date and its action; none
   outstanding and a payment due → the payment, "Make a payment"; neither → no band (§6.4). It
   never renders empty. **B3.2**: an outstanding document inside the configured window is visually
   escalated on its row and in the band — the window is configuration, not a constant, and the copy
   states the days remaining as it does now (the tone mechanism exists; the finding is that 13 and 41
   days render alike).
7. **F10 — the fold.** On any tab, the first block of that tab's own content is visible without
   scrolling at 1440×900. The space comes from 3 and 6; the banner and the card are not resized.
8. **F11–F15 — hierarchy, last.** "2 more ways to lower this" takes `--fs-h2`; the cost-of-attendance
   figure leaves the navigation step; actionable document rows take `--fs-h2`/`--fw-regular` (F12);
   coverage figures take `--fs-display`/`--fw-bold` and the deposit drops to the payment-figure
   level so `--fs-figure` is unused on this screen (F13, 9.0); "Still open to you" takes
   `--fs-small` + `--ls-caps` (F14); nothing collapses (F15).
