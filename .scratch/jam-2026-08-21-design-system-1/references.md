# References — My Documents inside Profile

Two Mobbin searches, `platform: web`, `mode: deep`, for the one shape this Jam adds: a section that
leaves the sidebar and lives inside the student's profile. The spacing and the sidebar order need no
reference — they are a token and a list.

## The entry point — a card on the profile that says what is there and opens it

- [Deel — person page](https://mobbin.com/screens/e0f2d8c2-6d46-4a77-9310-4992f9a88e1c) — **the shape
  we take.** The person's page lists its sub-areas as entry cards: icon, a name, one line saying what
  you will find (*Documents — Verify compliance. See if required documents are uploaded*), a chevron.
  The area itself is a page of its own. Our card carries the standing as well (what needs you, what
  is on record), because that is the one thing on Profile that can need action.
- [Deputy — onboarding checklist](https://mobbin.com/screens/0f96e0b6-c017-4b77-9c79-feb4010a41bb) —
  *Documents we need from you* ✓ / *Documents you need to sign or acknowledge*. Confirms the framing
  by who owes the move, which is how My Documents already cuts its two cards.
- [7shifts — onboarding documents](https://mobbin.com/screens/ddb0c7d4-44ab-47c5-946e-a7b13bbaa4ed) —
  a documents page that belongs to a profile: *Completed documents will be saved to the employee's
  profile*, with *Go to profile* beside the name. We take the relation, said in the eyebrow
  (`Profile · My Documents`) and in the sidebar: the profile chip is the active row while you are on
  documents.
- [Employment Hero — employee file](https://mobbin.com/screens/ee2d6c79-8859-4cba-a34a-39470986da9c)
  and [Airbnb — account settings › Taxes › Tax documents](https://mobbin.com/screens/8d23bf7e-ccad-4e6d-a8f5-fc2e3e81e9d6)
  — documents as a leaf of a person's record, reached from the record and not from the product's
  primary navigation. Confirms the Jam's move is a common one.

## Rejected

- [Aboard — Profile | Details | Documents tabs](https://mobbin.com/screens/6fd3a1ec-a015-4a48-a1a9-6380b6f25a8a)
  — documents as a tab under the profile head. Rejected by the shell's own rule: everything above a
  tab row is true of every leaf, and the documents figures (*2 need you · 4 on record*) are not true
  of the profile leaf. ADR 0002 and `PageShell` both lean on that rule.
- [Gusto — Documents as a sidebar item](https://mobbin.com/screens/2a4319d4-c9f9-40df-84ee-3a9023c180bd)
  — the shape we are leaving. Kept here so the decision is visible as a decision.
