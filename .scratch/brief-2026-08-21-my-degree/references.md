# References — My Degree changes (brief of 2026-08-21)

Four Mobbin searches, web platform, deep mode. The reference screen for form is My Enrollment in
this tree (`#/my-enrollment`); Mobbin was searched for the three shapes the brief adds that the
reference does not already carry: a course row with an action, a band with a button, and an
evidence panel that offers no verdict.

## The requirement list as a decision aid (D3, D6)

- [Coursera — course module](https://mobbin.com/screens/b7931f44-6fa5-4920-b892-c9bd340e496a) — a
  module header with what is left (*54 min of videos left · 1 graded assessment left*), then the
  items, and the first actionable one carries the only primary button (*Get started*) while the
  rest are plain rows. An info strip above says what credit the work earns, in conditional form.
  **We take**: the remaining line under the requirement head (D2), one primary per takeable row,
  and the conditional phrasing for anything not yet decided (D7).
- [Teachable — course curriculum](https://mobbin.com/screens/be865fb9-7894-4e03-8262-c14a16d810e3)
  — groups with a `0 / 3 complete` count in the head and a *Start* on every row. **We take the
  count on a closed head** (D16) and **reject** the button on every row: the brief puts the action
  only on courses the student can actually take.
- [Uxcel — syllabus with "Start here"](https://mobbin.com/screens/302c50f7-0ac2-4d8d-b435-e5bc9f4a855d)
  — a *Start here* callout on exactly one row of a long syllabus. **We take** the one-band rule:
  the band points at one thing and never renders with a placeholder (D5).
- [Vanta — audit progress](https://mobbin.com/screens/289ef10a-361a-4b7b-aabd-2e219e57ef2a) — one
  progress bar with its unit named beside it (*Audit progress*, then the counts). **We take** the
  named unit on the ring (D10).

## The evidence panel with no verdict control (D8)

- [TheyDo — insight detail, Evidence tab](https://mobbin.com/screens/0420156b-e923-44d7-9be9-8b86205fb7b5)
  — a side panel of label/value pairs (Type, Owner, Status, Origin) with an *Evidence* section
  underneath. **We take** the label/value reading for *What Aster read* and the order: facts, then
  the evidence, then who owns it.
- [Dovetail — suggested highlights](https://mobbin.com/screens/0d5151ab-6ade-47f5-8b03-024571200782)
  and [Grammarly — review suggestion](https://mobbin.com/screens/b727d76e-6475-4a20-a8bd-d0ad05569b30)
  — both put *Accept* / *Dismiss* on the suggestion. **Rejected**: rule 4 of the brief and R1 —
  the student is not a party to the decision, so the drawer offers no verdict control, only a route
  to the office that decides.

## Rejected for the list

- [Wrike — my to-do](https://mobbin.com/screens/7ba6ac77-8b13-4b63-a1e4-e1f9a453b4fb) — rows
  grouped by status only (*Planned*, *In review*, *Not started*). Status alone is what the brief
  calls "ordered by catalog, not by situation": the groups here are what the student can do
  (take now, later, blocked), not what state a row is in.
- [Codecademy — certification syllabus](https://mobbin.com/screens/de46de43-eeb1-4ec2-9ca0-bd4d85bd8bed)
  — numbered units with *Expand all*; every row identical. The brief's D3 is the opposite: the
  label says the situation and only the takeable rows carry the action.
