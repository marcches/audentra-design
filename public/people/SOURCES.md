# People — provenance

Every face in this folder is a **stock portrait from Unsplash**, downloaded once on 2026-08-21
from `images.unsplash.com` with the CDN's face-aware square crop (`fit=crop&crop=faces`, 512×512)
and resized to 256×256 WebP, quality 82. They are published under the
[Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license): free to use for commercial and non-commercial
purposes, no permission needed, attribution appreciated — so each one is credited below where the
photographer is known. No file depicts anyone who has a relationship with Aster or Audentra; the
names in the data are fictional and were assigned to the pictures, not the other way round.

## Who gets a photo, and how one is chosen

**A person Aster knows** — the student, an advisor, a club contact — has a photo on the record;
`Avatar` shows it, and shows initials when a record has none. Offices, residences, organisations
and other sites never get a face.

**The picture is chosen for the role, the way a real directory photo reads.** An advisor looks like
staff — thirties to fifties, dressed for an office, a plain or workplace background. A faculty
adviser can look like faculty. A student looks college-age. A club officer is a student. Never a
generated face: on 2026-08-21 a GAN generator, asked for a 35–50-year-old Admissions advisor,
returned the same teenager three times, and Marco read it as a child in a staff role. Stock
portraits of real adults, picked by hand for the role, are the rule since.

To replace one: pick from Unsplash with the role in mind, fetch
`https://images.unsplash.com/<id>?w=512&h=512&fit=crop&crop=faces&q=85`, resize to 256, keep the
file name — every record points at the name. Record the id and the photographer here.
