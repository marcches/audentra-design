# People — provenance

Every face in this folder is **synthetic**: generated by a GAN at
https://this-person-does-not-exist.com and downloaded once on 2026-08-21 through its
`/new?gender=&age=&etnic=` endpoint, so that the cast matches the names and roles the data gives
them. No file depicts a real person. The site offers the images for free download and publishes no
licence text beyond that; this note is the record of where each came from. Each original was
1024×1024; the central 72% was kept (the watermark rides a corner) and resized to 256×256 WebP.

Photos are for **people Aster knows** — the student and its staff. Offices, residences,
organisations and other sites never get a face (`docs/agents/design-workflow.md`, People).

Picked for the look of a US university directory: frontal, plain background, no hat or sunglasses,
staff in their 30s–50s, students college-age. The generator caches one image per parameter set for
a long while, so the pool per person was small; each pick below is the best of what it returned.
