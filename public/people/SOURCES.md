# People — provenance

One face lives here, and it is **synthetic**: generated by a GAN at
https://this-person-does-not-exist.com and downloaded once on 2026-08-21 through its
`/new?gender=&age=&etnic=` endpoint. No file depicts a real person. The site offers the images for
free download and publishes no licence text beyond that; this note is the record of where it came
from. The original was 1024×1024; the central 72% was kept (the watermark rides the top edge) and
resized to 256×256 WebP, quality 82.

## Who gets a photo

**The student, and only the student.** The portal holds her photo because she gave it one — the
ID-card step in onboarding — and her own face is what a student portal shows beside her own name.
Staff and contacts — the enrollment advisor, the financial aid advisor, a course advisor, a club's
faculty adviser — have no photo on the record and are drawn as initials by `Avatar`. That is also
what a real enrollment system has on file: it does not carry a directory of staff portraits.

This was not always the rule. For an afternoon on 2026-08-21 eleven faces lived here, one per
person the data names, and the generator — which caches one image per parameter set and ignores
the age it is asked for on some of them — returned a teenager for the Admissions advisor. Marco
read it as a child in a staff role and asked why staff had photos at all. They do not, now.
